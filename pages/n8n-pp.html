Privacy Policy for swe-n8n

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

swe-n8n is a private, self-hosted workflow automation integration designed exclusively for personal use by its owner. This Privacy Policy explains how Google user data is accessed, used, and protected.

1. Information Accessed

When authorized by the account owner via Google OAuth, swe-n8n accesses Gmail data (such as email metadata, headers, and message content) solely to trigger and execute automated personal workflows.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Data is processed strictly within the owner's private, self-hosted n8n instance to automate personal email triggers and actions.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning/AI models.

3. Data Storage and Security

Authentication tokens and workflow execution logs are stored locally on the owner's private infrastructure. Access is restricted to the owner, and communication with Google APIs occurs over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

swe-n8n's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention and Revocation

You may revoke swe-n8n's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account Permissions page. Upon revocation, stored tokens are invalidated and can be deleted from the self-hosted environment.
